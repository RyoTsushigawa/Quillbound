# Dramatic moments and public dice

All player dice, including defense rolls, are public on the connected player page as soon as they are rolled. Physical dice can also be entered. Cancelling an outcome or Undo does not erase the public roll history. The DM secret roller is separate and never enters that feed. The latest twenty player rolls are retained; the player page shows the latest eight. This remains a same-browser player screen, not remote player accounts.

The selected action chooses its ability and proficiency from the current character sheet. There are no Use or Trained controls. Attack and Help use the class's primary ability, Move uses Dexterity, and Defend uses the better of Dexterity and Constitution. Common improvised conditions choose a fitting ability from the sheet. A sheet's `combatActions` entries can explicitly specify an action's ability and training. No AI text inference is involved.

The DM can enable random dramatic moments and toggle More dramatic moments. Normal chance is 10%; heightened chance is 25%, for heroes and enemies equally. These are separate random percentile checks, not changes to d20 probabilities. The d20 still has exactly equal chances for each face; natural 1s and 20s keep their original meaning.

On a newly full wound track, Last Stand can keep a combatant in the fight with one wound box remaining. This is resistance to being taken out, not resurrection after established death. After a wounded or hindered combatant lands an action, Power Through can restore one wound and clear one hindering condition. There is no player activation or benefit-selection button. At most one successful dramatic moment occurs per combatant per encounter, with at most one check of each type per round. Only confirmed combat exchanges trigger these events; manual wound adjustments are corrections, not trigger attempts. Undo restores the entire exchange; replaying an exchange can cause another chance check, so the DM should use it for corrections rather than fishing for a proc.

Conditions have a description and a clearing instruction. Click a condition's information button to read them; its separate × button removes it. The Conditions guide includes standard conditions and combat statuses. Custom conditions can have their own descriptions. When combat sharing is enabled, those condition descriptions and triggered moments also reach the player screen. DM notes, secret rolls and trigger settings stay private.
