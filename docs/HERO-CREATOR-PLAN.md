# Hero creator: two-panel, guided (plan)

Nothing has been built yet. The structure follows the Dungeon Master's Vault builder: choices on the left with a counter of open choices, and a live sheet on the right. The design, wording and code are Quillbound's own, and the content is SRD only.

## Where we start

The current creator (`vCreate` in `index.html`) has five beginner steps: Hero type, Ancestry, Story, Abilities, Sheet.

- It has 10 classes, written in plain language with difficulty labels.
- It has 8 ancestries.
- It uses one recommended ability array, with a click-to-swap.
- Story prompts become leads on the DM desk.
- Every hero is level 1.
- It has no skills, saving throws, equipment, spell choices or subclasses.
- Its AC is a fixed number per class.
- The combat attack is a generic starter per class (`QBAttacks.STARTER`).

It already mixes 2014 and 2024 ideas: it has the Orc species, and it says the +2/+1 comes from the background.

## The layout

```
┌──────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────┐
│ Species ● Class ● Abilities  │  Wren · Level 3 Elf Wizard     │
│ ● Background ② Skills ② ...  │  [Summary][Combat][Skills]     │
│                              │  [Spells][Features][Gear]      │
│  ┌ choice cards ───────────┐ │                                │
│  │ plain-language pitch,    │ │  HP 17 · AC 12 · Speed 30     │
│  │ "You get: …", difficulty │ │  Attack: Fire Bolt +5, 1d10   │
│  └──────────────────────────┘ │  Stamina 2 · Hit Dice 3 × d6   │
│  Why this matters (1 line)   │  ⚠ 2 skill choices still open  │
│  [Back]            [Next]    │                                │
└──────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Left: guided steps.**
  - The steps are Species, Class & Level, Ability Scores, Background, Skills & Proficiencies, Spells, Equipment, Story, Review.
  - Each step tab shows a badge with its open choices ("②") and turns into a check mark when nothing is open.
  - Steps that don't apply are hidden; for example, a Fighter has no Spells step.
- **Right: the live sheet.** It updates on every click and has six tabs:
  - **Summary:** abilities, proficiency, passive Perception, speed, senses.
  - **Combat:** what Stakes uses, meaning HP, AC, attacks with their bonus and damage, stamina and Hit Dice.
  - **Skills:** all 18 skills and the saving throws.
  - **Spells**, **Features** and **Gear**.

  A warning line lists anything still open.
- **Beginner first.**
  - Every step has a **Recommended** choice and a one-line "why this matters".
  - **Quick build** lets a new player pick only a class and a name; every other choice is filled with a sensible default they can change later.
- **Phone width:** the sheet becomes a "View sheet" drawer at the bottom.

## How it works inside

- **Choices are stored and the sheet is derived.**
  - A hero keeps what was chosen: species, class levels, ability method, background, skill picks, spells and gear.
  - A new `src/app/hero-rules.js` turns those choices into the sheet with pure functions.
  - So you can change your mind, level up later, and test every number.
- **Fair dice:** "Roll 4d6, drop the lowest" for ability scores and rolled HP per level use the same rejection-sampled dice as combat.
- **Stakes uses the real sheet:**
  - Attacks come from the equipped weapons or attack cantrips, with Extra Attack and Sneak Attack as now.
  - AC comes from armor, shield and Dexterity.
  - HP, Hit Dice and proficiency (which sets stamina) come from the class levels.
  - The generic starter attack only remains for heroes made before the builder.
- **Existing heroes stay as they are.** Their sheet gets a "Rebuild in the new creator" button that pre-fills the choices it can infer.

## Data

The SRD files come from the same project as the monster list (github.com/5e-bits/5e-database). I'll ask before downloading anything.

| Needed for | 2024 file (SRD 5.2) | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Species, subspecies, traits | Species, Subspecies, Traits | 79 KB |
| Classes, levels, features, subclasses | Classes, Levels, Features, Subclasses | 600 KB |
| Backgrounds, feats | Backgrounds, Feats | 25 KB |
| Skills, proficiencies | Skills, Proficiencies | 39 KB |
| Equipment, weapon mastery | Equipment, Weapon-Mastery-Properties | 195 KB |
| Spells | Spells | 495 KB |

A converter (like `tools/srd-convert.cjs`) keeps only what the builder needs. Spells are loaded only when the Spells step opens.

## Build order

1. **Data and rules engine. Done 2026-10-28; not shown in the app yet.**
   - **Data:** `src/app/srd-heroes.js` (249 KB) and `src/app/srd-spells.js` (332 KB), generated by `tools/hero-srd-convert.cjs` from the archived 5e-bits/5e-database (`src/2024/en`).
   - **Engine:** `src/app/hero-rules.js` turns stored choices into the sheet.
   - **Check:** `tools/hero-check.cjs` tests five hand-calculated builds (Fighter 1, Wizard 5, Barbarian 3, Rogue 5, Monk 1), every class at levels 1–20, and fair dice.
   - Steps 2–4 (the two-panel creator for levels 1–20 with skills, equipment and spells), plus the combat link from step 5 for newly built heroes, were done and published on 2026-10-28 as version 9.
   - Still to do:
     - "Rebuild in the new creator" for older heroes.
     - Homebrew (step 6).
   - **Original plan for this step:**
   - Convert the SRD data and write `hero-rules.js` with derived numbers.
   - A new `hero-check` compares known example builds against hand-computed sheets: HP, AC, attack bonus, saves, skills and spell slots.
2. **Two-panel creator for level 1.** Species, Class, Abilities (standard array, point buy, fair 4d6 roll), Background with its origin feat and ability increases, Story, Review, and Quick build.
3. **Skills, equipment and spells,** each with open-choice counters and filtered, plain-language lists.
4. **Levels 2–20 and level-up:** hit points (average or a fair roll), subclass at the right level, ability score improvements or feats, and new spells.
5. **Combat link-up:** Stakes takes attacks and AC from the sheet, and existing heroes get "Rebuild".
6. **Homebrew** (from your earlier request): your own species, background or class, built inside a framework that shows how it compares with the SRD options.

Every step ends with `npm test`, a browser check, updated RULES.md and republishing the link.

## Decided (2026-10-28)

1. **Rules edition: 2024 (SRD 5.2).** It matches the species and background increases the app already uses. The monster list stays 2014 for now; the two are compatible.
2. **Levels 1–20 in the first release.** Steps 2–4 above ship together: a hero can be created at any level with subclass, ability score improvements or feats, and spells.
3. **Ability scores, four methods:**
   - **Set values:** type in scores you already have, for example rolled at the table or given by the DM, and the creator checks the 3–20 range.
   - Standard array.
   - Point buy (27 points).
   - Roll 4d6, drop the lowest, with fair dice, logged once per hero.
4. **Hit points per level:** the player chooses. The average is the default; a fair roll is logged.
